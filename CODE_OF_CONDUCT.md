# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a welcoming, harassment-free space for everyone, whatever their experience, background or identity.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive. Assume good intent.
- Give and accept feedback gracefully, and focus on what is best for the project.
- Help newcomers.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, discrimination.
- Publishing other people's private information.
- Trolling, spam, or anything that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour breaks these rules.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer through the private channel on their GitHub profile ([@91kr7](https://github.com/91kr7)). Reports are handled confidentially.
